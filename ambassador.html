ZATEEMEE / THE AMBASSADOR PROGRAM

Share the beauty of being yourself.

For brides, creators, stylists and wedding voices who believe a gown should tell a personal story. Bring your point of view to Zateemee and help others discover the atelier.

INDIVIDUALITY / ARTISTRY / FOREVER

THE IDEA

Real stories. Distinctive voices.

Zateemee is shaped by Nigerian artistry and by the individuality of the people who wear and share its work. This program is a way to explore thoughtful partnerships around bridal style, special occasions and the moments that stay with us.

WHO WE WOULD LOVE TO MEET

Find your place in the story.

01

Brides & clients

Share your own Zateemee experience and the details that made the look yours.

02

Creators & storytellers

Bring a considered visual voice to fashion, bridal style or meaningful celebrations.

03

Wedding professionals

Stylists, planners, photographers and other collaborators who connect people with exceptional design.

CREATIVE POSSIBILITIES

Stories worth sharing.

A partnership can take many forms. What matters is a perspective that feels true to you and respectful of the craft.

  • 01

    The bridal journey

    From first inspiration to the details chosen for a personal look.

  • 02

    A styled moment

    An editorial, celebration or creative interpretation of Zateemee design.

  • 03

    Introducing the atelier

    Thoughtful recommendations that connect people with the right experience.

THE PROCESS

A considered way to work together.

Every collaboration starts with a conversation. We review your work, your audience or community, and the kind of story you want to tell.

  1. 01 / INTRODUCE YOURSELF

    Tell us who you are, where we can see your work, and why Zateemee feels like a fit.

  2. 02 / EXPLORE THE FIT

    If there is a suitable opportunity, the atelier will get in touch to discuss an idea together.

  3. 03 / AGREE THE DETAILS

    Creative expectations, content use and any compensation or product arrangements are confirmed before work begins.

YOUR INTRODUCTION

Let’s make something memorable.

Tell us about your perspective. A thoughtful introduction is more useful than a polished pitch.

Applying does not guarantee a partnership. Any opportunity and its terms will be discussed individually.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few details before you apply.

What kinds of collaborations are possible?

These may include personal stories, original content, editorial projects or introductions to the atelier. The exact scope depends on the opportunity and is agreed with each collaborator.

Is this a paid role?

There is no standard public compensation offer. If a potential collaboration involves payment, product or other benefits, the atelier will explain the terms before you agree to participate.

Do I need a portfolio?

A portfolio or social profile helps us understand your perspective, but you can still introduce yourself if your story is best shared in words.

What happens after I apply?

The atelier reviews introductions and contacts applicants when there is a suitable opportunity to discuss. Please keep your contact information current.
