THE IDEA

A circle of trusted voices.

Many Zateemee brides find us through someone they trust. The Ambassador Programme rewards the women who introduce clients to the atelier, wherever they are in the world, with a commission on every qualifying order.

WHO WE WOULD LOVE TO MEET

Find your place in the story.

02

03

Wedding professionals

Stylists, planners and photographers who connect their clients with exceptional bridal and evening design.

WHAT YOU EARN

Your introductions, rewarded.

  • 01

    5% commission

    5% of the net dress value on each qualifying bridal and evening / special-event order you introduce.

  • 02

    Protected introductions

    Your referral is credited to you from first contact, for the period set out in the programme terms.

  • 03

    International payouts

    Paid in USD, EUR or GBP by PayPal or bank transfer, within 7–14 days of the order qualifying.

  • 04

    A welcome for your client

    Every client you introduce receives a welcome perk from the atelier.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps to your first commission.

  1. 01 / APPLY

    Tell us who you are, where you are based and how you connect with brides and clients.

  2. 02 / RECEIVE YOUR CODE

    Once approved, you receive your personal referral code, your ambassador toolkit and the full programme terms.

  3. 03 / INTRODUCE CLIENTS

    Share your code. Your client quotes it when she books her consultation, so the introduction is credited to you.

  4. 04 / GET PAID

    When her order qualifies, you earn 5% of the net dress value, paid within 7–14 days.

APPLY

Become a Zateemee ambassador.

Tell us about yourself and how you connect with brides and clients. The atelier reviews every application personally.

Ambassadors are selected by the atelier, and applying does not guarantee acceptance. Terms and conditions apply. Request programme terms and conditions.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few details before you apply.

How much do I earn?

5% of the net dress value on every qualifying bridal and evening / special-event order placed by a client you introduce. Terms and conditions apply.

What is net dress value?

The price of the dress after any discounts, excluding shipping, taxes, duties and alterations.

Which orders qualify?

Bridal and evening / special-event dress orders from new clients you introduce. Wholesale and boutique orders are not included.

How and when am I paid?

In USD, EUR or GBP by PayPal or bank transfer, within 7–14 days of the order qualifying.

Do I need to disclose that I am an ambassador?

Yes. Ambassadors must clearly disclose their relationship with Zateemee whenever they share or recommend the atelier.

Do I need a portfolio?

No. A social profile helps us get to know you, but you can apply without one.

What happens after I apply?

The atelier reviews every application and replies by email. Approved ambassadors receive their referral code, toolkit and full terms.
