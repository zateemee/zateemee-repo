BECOME A ZATEEMEE STOCKIST

Bring Zateemee to your bride.

Zateemee partners with select bridal boutiques worldwide to offer brides sculpted silhouettes, hand embellishment and couture detail rooted in Nigerian artistry. Following our presentation at the New York Bridal Market by One Fine Day, we are welcoming new stockist partners for the coming season.

Why Zateemee

A distinct point of view

Nigerian artistry and sculptural silhouettes that give your floor something brides will not find elsewhere.

Proven international demand

Brides in more than 11 countries, including the United States, United Kingdom, Ghana and the United Arab Emirates. Presented at the New York Bridal Market by One Fine Day in April 2026, and included in The Knot’s 29 Black Wedding Dress Designers You Should Know and Munaluchi Bride’s 25 Black Gown Designers for Your Wedding, Luxury Event, or Milestone Celebration Attire.

Couture craftsmanship

Corsetry, hand-placed lace and hand-applied embellishment are hallmarks of the collection.

The collection

The Forever Collection: Anita, Asa, Candace, Esther, Janice, Selena, Simi.
