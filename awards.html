AWARDS & MILESTONES

Moments that
shaped Zateemee

Zateemee’s story is one of steady evolution — from a Port Harcourt fashion brand to an increasingly international bridal house. The milestones below distinguish career achievements, business recognition and international market activity.

  1. 2010

    The beginning

    Mercy Ngozi Udoh begins her professional journey in fashion after a successful modelling career.

  2. 2012

    A defining breakthrough

    Mercy designs the winning gown for Miss Rivers State, marking an important early breakthrough in her design career.

  3. 2014

    The house is established

    Zateemee Designs is established in Port Harcourt and begins its evolution into the bridal house it is today.

  4. 2018

    Best Designer

    Zateemee receives the Best Designer recognition at the Golden Pageant Awards.

  5. 2019

    Top 100 Emerging SMEs

    Zateemee is recognised by Connect Nigeria among its Top 100 Emerging SMEs.

  6. 2022

    Fashion Gala Award

    Mercy receives a Fashion Gala Award recognising her contribution to fashion.

  7. 2025

    A Brides Expo — Fort Lauderdale

    Zateemee expands its international presence through participation in A Brides Expo in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

  8. 2026

    New York Bridal Market

    Zateemee presents at New York Bridal Market by One Fine Day on April 8–9, 2026, connecting with bridal buyers and industry professionals.

GLOBAL REACH

From Nigeria.
