Zateemee

Zateemee is a Nigerian luxury bridal house creating expressive, meticulously crafted gowns for the modern bride — where African heritage, contemporary design and exceptional craftsmanship meet.

Born in Port Harcourt, Zateemee has grown from a Nigerian fashion brand into an increasingly international bridal house. Founded by designer and creative director Mercy Ngozi Udoh, the house has developed a distinctive identity rooted in femininity, grandeur and the art of making a woman feel extraordinary.

The Zateemee story

Zateemee was created from a desire to make women feel beautiful, confident and unforgettable.

From the beginning, the house has approached fashion as more than clothing. It is an expression of identity, occasion and emotion — an understanding that what a woman wears can become part of how she remembers one of the most important moments of her life.

That philosophy lives in every Zateemee gown.

Sculpted silhouettes. Luxurious fabrics. Intricate embellishment. Dramatic details.

Each element is considered not simply for beauty, but for the presence it creates.

Zateemee draws inspiration from the richness of Nigerian ceremonial dressing and translates that grandeur through a contemporary luxury vocabulary. The result is a style that feels distinctly African without being confined by tradition — sophisticated, feminine and unmistakably Zateemee.

From Nigeria, to the world

What began in Port Harcourt has steadily expanded beyond Nigeria.

Zateemee gowns have reached brides in more than 11 countries, including the United States, United Kingdom, Ghana and the United Arab Emirates. The house has also taken its collections into international bridal markets, including A Brides Expo in Fort Lauderdale and New York Bridal Market by One Fine Day in 2026.

Today, Zateemee is building a new chapter — developing collections for a growing network of bridal boutiques and brides internationally, while preserving the bespoke couture experience that allows the house to create on a more personal level.
