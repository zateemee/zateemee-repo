The Zateemee story.

CHAPTER I / PORT HARCOURT

Where the story begins.

Born in Port Harcourt, Zateemee is a Nigerian luxury bridal house founded by designer and creative director Mercy Ngozi Udoh. African heritage, contemporary design and exceptional craftsmanship meet in expressive gowns for the modern bride.

CHAPTER II / THE HOUSE

Made to be remembered.

Zateemee was created from a desire to make women feel beautiful, confident and unforgettable. Each gown is an expression of identity, occasion and emotion, made for a moment that becomes part of her story.

CHAPTER III / THE SIGNATURE

Artistry with presence.

Sculpted silhouettes. Luxurious fabrics. Intricate embellishment. Dramatic details. The ceremonial elegance of Nigerian dressing is interpreted through a contemporary luxury vocabulary, balancing femininity, grandeur and couture craft.

CHAPTER IV / THE WORLD

From Nigeria, to the world.

Zateemee gowns have reached brides in more than 11 countries, including the United States, United Kingdom, Ghana and the United Arab Emirates. The house presented at A Brides Expo in Fort Lauderdale in 2025 and the New York Bridal Market by One Fine Day in April 2026.
