OUR STORY

Rooted in artistry.
Created for individuality.

Zateemee Bride is the bridal expression of designer Mercy Ngozi Udoh’s vision: elegance, individuality and presence. Drawing on the refined flamboyance of her Nigerian roots, The Forever Collection brings that vision to brides around the world.

Our design language moves between sculpted satin and dimensional lace, clean lines and intricate hand embellishment. A gown should feel as distinctive as the woman who chooses it.

THE DETAILS THAT DEFINE US

The art of the gown.

Structure

Considered corsetry, defined waists and precision draping shape the silhouette.

Detail

Hand-placed lace, appliqué, crystal and pearl embellishments give each design its character.

Expression

Detachable trains, bows and overskirts bring versatility to selected gowns in the collection.
